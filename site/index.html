    How this keeps recordings private
    • Recordings are transcribed inside this browser, on this Chromebook.
    • The app is blocked from contacting any other website, so audio and text cannot be sent anywhere.
    • Files you choose from the recorder are not copied or kept. Close the tab and they are gone from the app.
    • Recordings made here are saved on this Chromebook as they record, encrypted with a key that never leaves this browser. They stay until you press Delete.
    • Saved transcripts and recordings go where you choose. Keep them on this Chromebook, not in Google Drive or another online folder.
