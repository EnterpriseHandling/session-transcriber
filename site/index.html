2 Transcribe

Quality

3 Check and save

Read it through against the recording and correct any mistakes. You can type straight into the box.

How this keeps recordings private
  • The recording is read and transcribed inside this browser, on this device.
  • The app is blocked from contacting any other website, so audio and text cannot be sent anywhere.
  • Nothing is kept by the app. Close the tab and the recording and transcript are gone unless you saved them.
  • Saved transcripts go where you choose. Keep them on this device, not in a shared or online folder.
  • It works with no internet connection once it has been opened once online.

Transcripts are a machine's best attempt and will contain mistakes. Always check them against the recording.
